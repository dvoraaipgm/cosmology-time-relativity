## 1. Cosmic Structure & Galactic Distribution Paradoxes

### 1.1 The "Galaxies Not Dispersed into the Infinite Void" Problem
* **Standard Cosmology Dilemma:** In an infinitely expanding universe (or one expanding indefinitely driven by Dark Energy), matter should continuously disperse over gigayear timescales. Galaxies, clusters, and filaments should dissipate into vast voids, leading to thermodynamic heat death and structural dilution.
* **CCM Resolution:** The closed $14.23 \text{ Gly}$ container imposes a physical structural boundary with an equilibrium potential well. Matter cannot escape into an infinite vacuum; instead, central supermassive black holes continuously recycle old stellar material via superradiant dispersal. ### 1.2 The 360-Degree Spatial Dipole & Universal Symmetry
* **Standard Cosmology Dilemma:** Mainstream astronomy struggles to explain why the observable universe appears almost perfectly isotropic across 360 degrees (e.g., the uniform Cosmic Microwave Background temperature down to 1 part in $10^5$), while local observations show severe spatial asymmetries (e.g., the Cosmic Dipole anomaly, Great Attractor pull, and directional alignment of quasar polarization vectors).
* **CCM Resolution:** The $360^\circ$ baseline isotropy is the natural structural equilibrium of the spherical container's global gravitational potential. The local directional asymmetries (such as the Gaia parallax offsets) are not global cosmological flaws—they are the predictable geometric signature of an observer (Earth) positioned $8.0 \text{ kpc}$ off-center relative to the primary local density node (Sagittarius A*).

### 1.3 The Unexplained "Void Phenomenon" & Large-Scale Filaments
* **Standard Cosmology Dilemma:** $N$-body cold dark matter simulations fail to reproduce the sharp, empty cosmic voids and thin, hyper-dense galactic walls observed in redshift surveys (e.g., SDSS, 2dFGRS). Standard gravity predicts voids should contain far more dwarf galaxies than observed.
* **CCM Resolution:** Non-linear light propagation and local density gradients dictate that photons passing through low-density voids experience less compounding deceleration. Distances across voids are systematically overestimated in standard redshift space, making empty regions appear artificially enlarged while galaxy filaments are naturally confined by container density nodal lines.

## 2. Astrometric & Cosmological Observation Paradoxes

### 2.1 The $5\sigma$ Hubble Tension Crisis
* **Standard Cosmology Dilemma:** High-precision local measurements using Cepheids and Supernovae Ia ($H_0 \approx 73.04 \text{ km/s/Mpc}$) contradict deep-space early-universe measurements from Planck CMB data ($H_0 \approx 67.40 \text{ km/s/Mpc}$) at a statistical significance exceeding $5\sigma$.
* **CCM Resolution:** The "tension" is a mathematical artifact caused by fitting a linear formula ($v = H_0 d$) to a non-linear gravitational gradient. $H_0$ is re-defined as the local slope derivative:
$$H_{0,\text{apparent}}(d) = H_{\text{global}} + (H_{\text{local}} - H_{\text{global}}) \cdot e^{-\frac{d}{d_c}}$$
Local surveys sample the steep initial slope, while deep CMB surveys sample the flat asymptotic tail near the container boundary.

### 2.2 Persistent Gaia Parallax Zero-Point Offsets (PZPO)
* **Standard Cosmology Dilemma:** ESA’s Gaia spacecraft data exhibits systematic parallax errors ($-17 \ \mu\text{as}$ to $-38 \ \mu\text{as}$) that vary across magnitude, color, and galactic coordinates. Standard models dismiss these as instrumental calibration glitches.
* **CCM Resolution:** The offsets represent the physical footprint of directional light deceleration. Photons arriving along vectors pointing toward Sagittarius A* traverse a denser gravitational field, suffering greater wavefront compression than photons arriving from the outer anticenter.

### 2.3 The Dark Energy / Accelerating Universe Illusion
* **Standard Cosmology Dilemma:** Type Ia Supernovae at $z \sim 0.5 - 1.0$ appear systematically dimmer than predicted by a decelerating expanding universe. Standard cosmology invented "Dark Energy" ($\Lambda \approx 68\%$ of cosmic mass-energy) to force space to accelerate.
* **CCM Resolution:** Photons suffer compounding energy loss ($E \propto (1+z)^{-1}$) as they navigate the container's density gradient over billions of light-years. Supernovae appear dimmer not because space is accelerating, but because photon energy drops off along a non-linear saturation curve near $R = 14.23 \text{ Gly}$. Zero Dark Energy is needed.

### 3.1 The "Impossible Early Galaxy" Paradox
* **Standard Cosmology Dilemma:** Deep-field observations by the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) revealed massive, fully-formed, chemically mature galaxies at $z > 10$ (e.g., GLASS-z12, JADES-GS-z14-0).
