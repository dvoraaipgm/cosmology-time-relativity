CCM Core Formulas

Earth Baseline: $c_{\text{earth}} = 299,792.458 \text{ km/s}$ at $r_{\text{earth}} = 150 \text{ Mpc}$.

Center Speed: $c_{\text{center}} = c_{\text{earth}} \cdot e^{-r_{\text{earth}}/\alpha} = 289,274.52 \text{ km/s}$.

Velocity Gradient: $v(r) = c_{\text{earth}} \cdot e^{(r - r_{\text{earth}}) / \alpha}$ ($\alpha = 4200 \text{ Mpc}$).

Redshift Distance: $r(z) = \alpha \cdot \ln(1 + z)$.
